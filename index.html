About OBMC

Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center (OBMC) is a school that helps students develop independence, discipline, responsibility, and knowledge. It provides education from early childhood to senior high school.

Through Montessori education, students learn to become responsible individuals, explore their abilities, and prepare for their future.

Our Goal

To help students become responsible, respectful, and independent members of society.
